# Communities — MVP brief (for Jess + Taisiya)

**The bet:** friendship forms through *repetition*, not matching. The whole category optimizes the first meeting; retention lives in the third. **Communities = a persistent, tooled home for a group that already clicked** — the retention engine nobody in the space has built (not TripBFF, not NomadTable).

---

## The wedge: Vibes → Communities (grow from gelled groups, never cold-start)
Do **not** launch a tab full of empty topic rooms — that's a ghost town and a different (much harder) game. Instead, **at the end of every Vibe**, prompt the host:

> "You all showed up — want to keep this group going?" → **Create a Community** → add the attendees → a persistent chat with tools.

Why this is the unlock:
- The group **already met IRL** → warm supply, real relationships, not strangers.
- **Safe** — regulars who know each other, not open-join-by-strangers (fits our familiarity-based safety story).
- The **Vibe's category** (art, sports, wine, music, books…) auto-selects the community's **tools**.
- Turns a one-off event into a *recurring* thing — the thesis, operationalized.

## Community vs Flock (the thing you asked about)
- **Community** = persistent, recurring, local, tooled group → **this is the conversion target for a Vibe.**
- **Flock** = a group *trip* (travel, a destination). A local Vibe shouldn't "become a Flock."
- Instead: a Community can later **plan a Flock** *as an action inside it* — travel as an **outcome of belonging**. (This also differentiates hard from TripBFF: they match strangers for one trip; we let a group that already gels go travel together.)

## The tools — one universal + one hero per category
**Universal (every community gets this, build FIRST):** **"Next meetup"** — schedule + RSVP the next gathering. This is the **rhythm engine**; a community with no next-meetup is a dead chat, and repetition is the entire point.

**Category hero tool (ship exactly ONE to start):**

| Vibe category | Community hero tool |
|---|---|
| Books | **Sync-read** — same book, chapter-gated discussion (no spoilers), shared quotes |
| Wine / food | Tasting notes + place/bottle log + ratings |
| Music | Collaborative playlist + gig calendar |
| Sports | Recurring sessions + RSVP + results/streaks |
| Art | Shared board + critique / feedback |
| Travel | Trip planner + expense **ledger** |

## Base toolkit — MSN-nostalgia (every community gets these)
The aesthetic: **MSN Messenger** — where you talked to your *actual friends*, not a feed of strangers. Warm, playful, presence-first — the belonging thesis in the right skin, and all **WhatsApp-can't** (reinforces the moat). Cheap, high-delight, universal:

| Tool | MSN DNA | Cost | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nudge / Buzz** | shake the chat + device buzz; rate-limited (MSN capped it) | Low | "oi, you there?" — the single most nostalgic thing |
| **Status quote** | a personal line/quote under your name | Low | identity + a reason to check in |
| **Now playing** | what you're listening to (manual or Spotify) | Low–med | passive intimacy, the group's mood |
| **Presence + away message** | online / away / busy + a custom away message | Low | makes the room feel *alive* |
| **Winks / animated reactions** | send a full-chat animation | Low–med | delight, "thinking of you" |
| **Join/leave chime** | the little "door" sound (toggleable) | Low | instant nostalgia, signals presence |
| **Group games** | trivia night, would-you-rather, two-truths-and-a-lie | Med | the *reason to gather* = the rhythm |

**MVP base pick:** Nudge + Status quote + Now-playing + Presence/away + a **weekly group Quiz** (with the collective-unlock below). That set alone makes a plain chat feel alive and *yours*.

**Tech note:** true haptic vibration is reliable on a **native app** + Android web; **iOS Safari has no vibration API** → on web the buzz is a visual shake + sound, full haptics on native. (Matters for the web-vs-app call.)

## Gamification — community vs. the platform (co-op)
The whole community collectively **"beats the platform"** in a game → **unlocks premium tools for free.** Why it's the smartest piece:
- **Co-op, not competitive** (community vs. "the house") → *bonds* the group instead of dividing it.
- **Collective + effortful-but-winnable goal** — e.g. a combined weekly trivia score, or a streak of N meetups. Not trivial, not impossible.
- **Reward = a *temporary* premium unlock** → gives the group a taste → natural upsell later. (Watch the margin: great early growth/engagement loop; tune what's given away once monetizing.)
- **Runs on a weekly beat** → the challenge *is* the "next meetup" recurrence engine, gamified. One mechanic, two jobs.

## MVP scope (ruthless — one vertical, one hero tool)
1. **Vibe-end → "Create Community"** prompt to the host → add attendees → persistent chat.
2. The universal **"Next meetup"** tool (the recurrence engine).
3. **One** category hero tool — pick the vertical we can *seed right now* (where we have hosts/crowd).
4. Instrument **3-week recurrence** (communities with activity 3+ weeks running, weekly-active members) as the **north-star — not signups**.

**Explicitly NOT in the MVP:** a wall of topic rooms · payments / combined wallet (ledger only — link out to Venmo/PayPal/UPI to settle; real money movement is a whole regulated company) · every-category tools at once · open public join.

## Open decisions for the session
- **Which first category** can we actually seed? (Determines the first hero tool.)
- **Opt-in:** host adds all attendees automatically, or each attendee opts in?
- **Naming:** "Community" vs something warmer.
- **First hero tool:** lean **Sync-read** (novel, very sticky) if books/local; **Trip planner** if travel-first.

## Rough build phases
- **P1 — the container:** Vibe-end conversion prompt · persistent Community chat · member management · the "Next meetup" tool.
- **P2 — the hook:** one category hero tool + category auto-detected from the Vibe.
- **P3 — prove & extend:** measure 3-week recurrence; if groups stay, add the 2nd category/tool and "Community → plan a Flock."

## The one-liner
Grow **one** deeply-tooled community type out of groups that already gelled (Vibe → Community), give every community a **rhythm** (next-meetup) plus **one** category hero tool, and win on **3-week recurrence** — not a wall of empty rooms with a suite of half-built tools.
